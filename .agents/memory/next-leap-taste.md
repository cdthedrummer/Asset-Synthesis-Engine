name: Next Leap taste profile (Charlie)
description: Charlie's locked product/design direction for Next Leap after the July 2026 taste-lock rounds
---
- Direction locked July 2026: Pinboard metaphor won (everything a card, pinned next moves, persistent input) over Thread and Feed.
- Pins = bespoke mini-infographics per topic — calendars, bar charts, step trackers, permit tables — with phone-health-app simplicity (one accent, no chart junk). NOT editorial one-liners. **Why:** Charlie: "less subtext, more visual — Visual Capitalist-like, but simple like a health app." **How to apply:** any new pin type gets a purpose-built glanceable visual, never a text blurb.
- No "coach" label anywhere in UI copy — chrome stays neutral, the voice carries the opinions. The approved v1 spec reinstated opinionated beats (plain verdicts, the abandon-bet, the check-in dodge call-out) as content, not persona branding. **Why:** Charlie rejected coach *branding* ("We're not a coach") but approved verdict-first copy and the bet beat in the v1 spec.
- Pins behave like ChatGPT projects: tap → closer view + pin-scoped chat with history; context-aware persistent input bar (board mode vs pin mode); quick pin-to-pin switching; Keep-style quick add (+ or ask chat).
- Next-moves list = the pinned projects in customizable priority order; every row links back to its pin.
- Dark mode was a v1 assumption — Charlie: "dark mode is for nerds." A restyle round (likely light) is owed once structure locks; do not ship the real product dark-first without revisiting.
- Board is near-wordless: ~80% fewer words than a labeled dashboard; words live in the chat, not on pins. No pin headlines — a snapshot must self-identify (cover the verdict chip and you still know what it is in a second). Numbers/dates fine; sentences/labels are the smell. **Why:** Charlie's failure-mode callout: "we look like an AI dashboard and not an engaging app."
- Pins sort by conversation recency, not verdict priority — "lead with whatever we spoke about most recently." Board mirrors the conversation; tiny recency stamps ok.
- Chat voice stays charlie-voice public register (direct, brief, contractions, no exclamations); the surfaces lean visual, the chat carries the voice.
- Demo personas: Maya (baker → bakery owner) and Dev (swim instructor → head coach). Demo boards are read-only by design; chat streams but never persists on them.
