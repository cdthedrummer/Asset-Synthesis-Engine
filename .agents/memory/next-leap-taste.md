---
name: Next Leap taste profile (Charlie)
description: Charlie's locked product/design direction for Next Leap after the July 2026 taste-lock rounds
---
- Direction locked July 2026: Pinboard metaphor won (everything a card, pinned next moves, persistent input) over Thread and Feed.
- Pins = bespoke mini-infographics per topic — calendars, bar charts, step trackers, permit tables — with phone-health-app simplicity (one accent, no chart junk). NOT editorial one-liners. **Why:** Charlie: "less subtext, more visual — Visual Capitalist-like, but simple like a health app." **How to apply:** any new pin type gets a purpose-built glanceable visual, never a text blurb.
- No "coach" label anywhere in UI copy — chrome stays neutral, the voice carries the opinions. The approved v1 spec reinstated opinionated beats (plain verdicts, the abandon-bet, the check-in dodge call-out) as content, not persona branding. **Why:** Charlie rejected coach *branding* ("We're not a coach") but approved verdict-first copy and the bet beat in the v1 spec.
- Pins behave like ChatGPT projects: tap → closer view + pin-scoped chat with history; context-aware persistent input bar (board mode vs pin mode); quick pin-to-pin switching; Keep-style quick add (+ or ask chat).
- Next-moves list = the pinned projects in customizable priority order; every row links back to its pin.
- Dark mode was a v1 assumption — Charlie: "dark mode is for nerds." **The restyle round shipped July 2026 as "Shortlist":** warm paper ground (`--paper #FBF8F3`), one saturated accent `--start #C4451C` terracotta reserved for START verdicts and begin-something CTAs, `--moss #4F6B3E` for the achievement layer, warm stone for SKIP. Fraunces display / Inter UI / IBM Plex Mono kickers, self-hosted. **How to apply:** terracotta = the invitation to act, moss = the receipt that you did; never spend the accent on anything else, or START stops being singular.
- Board is near-wordless: ~80% fewer words than a labeled dashboard; words live in the chat, not on pins. No pin headlines — a snapshot must self-identify (cover the verdict chip and you still know what it is in a second). Numbers/dates fine; sentences/labels are the smell. **Why:** Charlie's failure-mode callout: "we look like an AI dashboard and not an engaging app."
- Pins sort by conversation recency, not verdict priority — "lead with whatever we spoke about most recently." Board mirrors the conversation; tiny recency stamps ok.
- Chat voice stays charlie-voice public register (direct, brief, contractions, no exclamations); the surfaces lean visual, the chat carries the voice.
- Demo personas: Maya (baker → bakery owner) and Dev (swim instructor → head coach). Demo boards are read-only by design; chat streams but never persists on them — every mutation route must 403 with "Demo boards are read-only — start your own from the front door."
- Round one feedback (July 2026): Charlie is tap-first — offer chips over typing wherever a question has natural answers (interview multiple-choice, chat replies end with tap options, starter prompts on empty pin chats). Pins must be hands-on, not read-only: edit, "Add info" (AI folds new facts into the pin), delete, and Keep-style checklists live on every pin. Interview card sits on a solid scrim on mobile (translucent gradient stays desktop-only). **How to apply:** new conversational surfaces should ship with tap answers by default; new pin surfaces ship with the full hands-on action row.
- Engagement rework (July 2026): the promise is **triage relief** — "permission to drop things, an order to do the rest in, and the first one already done." The board is the receipt; the SKIP stamps and the abandon-bet are the product. **Why:** Charlie's own question, "what does the user get out of this," and his source material ("I feel as though I am leaving too much work on the cutting room floor"). **How to apply:** any new surface has to make dropping something feel like a result, not a gap.
- **The achievement layer IS the sensor.** This board borrows the phone-health-app look but not its physics: a health app is filled in by a sensor while you sleep, and this has no pedometer. Completed moves, ticked checklist items and check-ins are the only telemetry it can honestly collect, so they are what the board visualises — that's what keeps it alive on a Tuesday when nobody has typed for nine days. Progress is a **board-level element** (`PulseCard`), never a pin kind the model can place: pins carry facts the owner said, this carries facts the app observed. Week streaks only — a day streak is an app-opening reward with extra steps, and empty weeks render empty because the board is allowed to look quiet when the owner was quiet.
- Verdicts are not peers. START is the only saturated fill on the board; SKIP FOR NOW is a hairline stamp **and** sets the whole card down (`opacity-70`, no shadow), so a dropped pin reads as dropped with the chip covered. Emphasis only — sort order stays conversation recency.
- Effort descends while the board ascends. They typed the hard sentence to get in the door; question two is a tap. Interview capped at 5 and force-finished in code, `ask` types (single/multi/rank/scale/image) so answering keeps getting cheaper. Ranking is tap-to-order, never drag — dragging inside a bottom sheet over a scrolling board is the highest-failure interaction in the set.
- The interview docks, it does not curtain. The old overlay scrimmed the whole board on phones, so the owner answered every question with the thing they were building hidden behind frosted glass. No scrim at any breakpoint; the dock publishes its height as `--dock-h` so nothing is stranded under it.
- Reward copy is deterministic and lives in `reward-lines.ts` — no model call (a button press can't wait on a round trip, and a model asked to celebrate will gush). Motion celebrates; the words stay flat and specific. Dropping a move is a legitimate completion: "Dropped. That's a decision, not a gap."
- No accounts means the link is the account. Say so on the front door, offer a copy-link everywhere, remember the last real board in `localStorage` (never a demo), and make `document.title` the goal text so browser history can find a lost board. Email capture is the only thing that unlocks real re-engagement and Charlie has not decided on it — do not smuggle it in.
- Demo boards are the shop window, so they must look **complete**: every demo needs at least one skip pin (or the reveal has no payoff to show), checklist rows with `done_at` spread over weeks including one empty week, and `cycle_index` set on moves. Priya (`demo-care`, juggle door) exists because the juggle door had no shop window and both other demos read as small businesses.

- **SKIP FOR NOW is warm stone, not rose.** Rose was also the destructive/error token, so the board painted its most common good outcome in the error colour while the copy said "Dropped. That's a decision, not a gap." Red is now reserved for actual destruction. **How to apply:** a dropped pin also stops floating (`bg-paper`, no shadow) rather than being dimmed with `opacity-70` — opacity dimmed the stamp itself to 1.39:1, and a muted stamp cannot survive that where an alarm-red one could.
- **The board teaches its own notation once.** A bottom-sheet "key" (four stamps + `VERDICT_EXPLANATIONS`) opens from the app bar and auto-shows once per device right after the reveal. This is a map key, not a coach: it labels the notation, never the person, and it lives behind a tap so the resting board stays wordless.
- Progress is board-level and so is chrome: a 52px sticky app bar carries the mark (the only way home), the goal condensed on scroll, the key, copy-link and check-in. The mark alone, no wordmark — the front door is the one place the product says its own name.
