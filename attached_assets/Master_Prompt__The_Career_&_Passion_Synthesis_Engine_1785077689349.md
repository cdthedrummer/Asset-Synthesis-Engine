# **The Career & Passion Synthesis Engine**

This master prompt is designed to act as a **Strategic Career Architect**. It doesn't just look at what you've done; it looks for the "connective tissue" between your professional output (like your ROI scoring methodologies and OAI adoption work) and your personal creative sparks.

Copy and paste the block below into your preferred advanced LLM once you have gathered your project notes.

# **The Master Prompt**

## **Core Directive**

You are acting as a **Strategic Career Architect and Operations Consultant**. Your goal is to ingest a massive data dump of my work from the past year—including professional projects, personal hobbies, half-finished ideas, and career aspirations—and synthesize them into a unified "Great Model."

I feel as though I am leaving too much work on the cutting room floor and failing to connect disparate projects into a narrative that propels my career forward. I need you to find the "missed connections" that turn individual efforts into a cohesive, shareable, and useful system.

## **Phase 1: The Context Injection**

Here is the data for your analysis:

* **Professional Projects:** File  
* **Hobbies & Creative Passions:** Person (Describe what you do for fun/passion here)  
* **The "Cutting Room Floor":** (List the projects you've started but haven't published or finished)  
* **Current Sentiments:** (Describe your frustrations, your "gut feelings" about where you're stuck, and what "success" looks like to you)

## **Phase 2: Analytical Frameworks**

Analyze the provided data using the following lenses:

1. **Categorization:** Group projects by domain (e.g., Automation Rigor, Narrative Storytelling, Scalable Infrastructure).  
2. **The Shortcoming Audit:** Where are the gaps? Are projects failing because of lack of engineering support, lack of "packaging" for leadership, or lack of alignment with my passions?  
3. **Opportunity Synthesis:** Look for the "hidden bridges." For example, how does my hobby in Person apply to my professional work in Person?

## **Phase 3: Visual & Structured Output**

As a visual learner, I require the following formats for your response:

1\. The Strategic Quadrant (Priority Matrix)  
Generate a table (or a textual representation of a 2x2 grid) plotting every project based on:

* X-Axis: Difficulty to Complete (Low to High).  
* Y-Axis: Upside Opportunity/Impact (Low to High).  
* Labels: Categorize them into "Quick Wins," "Big Bets," "Fillers," and "Thankless Tasks."

2\. The "Great Model" Narrative  
In 3–5 paragraphs, explain how these projects connect to form a singular, powerful career identity. What is the "Red Thread" that ties my automation ROI work to my personal creative interests?

3\. The Priority Task List  
Provide a list of actions in strict order of importance. This list should focus on:

* What to finish and publish *immediately* to get a "shareable" win.  
* What to stop doing because it's a "shortcoming" or a "time sink."  
* How to "package" existing work so it is useful to friends and colleagues.

## **Phase 4: Tone & Style**

* Be objective but encouraging.  
* Use professional, high-level terminology (e.g., "Operating Rigor," "Capacity Release," "Governance Pathways").  
* Focus on **Productization**: How do I turn an "idea" into a "product" or "framework"?

# **How to use this prompt effectively**

| Component | Why it's included |
| :---- | :---- |
| **The Data Dump** | Models like Claude Fable need "raw material" to find patterns. Don't self-censor; include the messy notes. |
| **The "Red Thread"** | This specifically addresses your feeling of "missed connections." It forces the AI to find the bridge between ROI scoring and your hobbies. |
| **The Quadrant** | This satisfies your visual learning style and helps you triage what to "leave on the cutting room floor" intentionally. |
| **Productization Focus** | Since you want to "share" your work, the prompt asks the AI to think about how to package your work for an audience. |

## **Suggested Next Steps**

1. **Gather your "Cutting Room Floor" items:** Even if it's just a folder of titles or a few sentences on what the idea was.  
2. **Identify your "Passions":** Be specific. If you love 3D printing, music production, or gardening, mention it. The AI might find a way to apply "Video Creative Genome" logic to it.  
3. **Run the prompt:** Be prepared to ask follow-up questions like "Can you expand on the Quick Wins section with a 30-day plan?"
