"Laundry Lift" Dryer Attachment
Executive Summary
A lightweight laundry basket attachment, Laundry Lift allows you to scoop clothes out the dryer and directly into a laundry basket, leaving no small items left behind!
Mission Statement
To provide a simple and effective solution that makes it easier for people to do laundry.
Vision Statement
To be the leading provider of eco-friendly laundry solutions that make people's lives easier.
Product Description
The Laundry Lift is a lightweight, adjustable attachment that clamps to the open dryer's inside lip. It is made of durable plastic and features a scoop-like design that allows users to easily scoop clothes out of the dryer and directly into a laundry basket. The Laundry Lift is adjustable to fit different dryer sizes and is easy to install or remove.
Target Market
The Laundry Lift is targeted at people who want an easier way to remove clothes from the dryer, especially those with stackable washer/dryer combos. The target market includes:
* People who live in small spaces
* Families with young children
* Busy professionals
* Elderly or disabled individuals
Market Analysis
The laundry care market is a large and growing industry, with a global market size of over $100 billion. The market is expected to continue to grow in the coming years, driven by factors such as the increasing number of households and the rising demand for convenience.
Competition
There are a few existing products on the market that address the problem of removing clothes from the dryer. However, these products are often bulky, expensive, or difficult to use. The Laundry Lift is a unique product that offers a simple, effective, and affordable solution.
The Laundry Lift's Competitive Advantages:
* No Installation: Unlike tilt-out hampers or laundry drawers, Laundry Lift is easy to handle and remove.
* Simplicity: Its key focus is ease of unloading the dryer – a single, specific problem it solves well.
* Affordability: The concept suggests a potentially lower price point compared to larger-scale solutions.
* Adaptability: The adjustable nature means fitting a wider range of dryers than one-size-fits-all competitors.
Marketing Plan
The marketing plan for the Laundry Lift will focus on reaching the target market through a variety of channels, including:
* Online advertising
* Social media marketing
* Email marketing
* Public relations
* Trade shows and events
