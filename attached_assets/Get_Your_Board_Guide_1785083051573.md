# Get Your Board
### A step-by-step guide to figuring out what's next — with AI doing the heavy lifting

You have a goal — open the bakery, become the head coach, go independent — and a fog of "where do I even start." This walks you through turning that fog into a scored, ranked board with your next three moves on it. Takes about 30 minutes. No AI experience needed.

---

## Before you start (5 minutes, once)

1. Go to **claude.ai** and sign in (create a free account if you don't have one). A laptop or desktop browser works best — the board you'll get is interactive.
2. Install the skill — one of two ways:
   - **If you were given a `portfolio-impact.skill` file:** in Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** (or **Features**) → **Skills** → upload the file → save.
   - **Can't find that menu, or on a plan without skills?** No problem — open the `.skill` file's `SKILL.md` in any text editor, copy everything, and paste it as the *first message* of your chat with one line on top: *"Follow these instructions for our conversation:"* Works identically.
3. Open a **new chat**. Don't reuse an old one.

## Step 1 — Say what you're trying to become (2 min)

Type this, filled in with your real situation. Don't polish it; honest and messy beats impressive:

> **"I want to [your goal — e.g., go from home baker to owning a small bakery]. Run me through Reinvention Mode and build me my board. Interview me first — one question at a time."**

The "one question at a time" part matters — it keeps the interview from feeling like a tax form.

## Step 2 — Answer the interview honestly (10 min)

You'll get 5–8 questions: your 12-month picture, what you've already done, your real constraints (hours, money, non-negotiables), how much you've used AI, how you feel about promoting yourself, and what part you've been avoiding.

Rules for good answers:
- **Concrete beats aspirational.** "I want 10 wholesale accounts by next summer" beats "I want to be successful."
- **Confess the boring stuff.** The half-finished website, the certification you never scheduled, the fear of posting online. The board is only as honest as the input.
- **State real numbers.** Hours per week you actually have. Money you could spend without losing sleep. These set the difficulty scores.

## Step 3 — Get the board (2 min)

When the interview ends, it will build your board — a visual with every workstream of your goal plotted by *how hard it is for you* vs. *how much it moves your 12-month picture*, each one stamped **START**, **SCHEDULE**, **SKIP FOR NOW**, or **GET HELP**, with a first move and the exact AI prompts to run each step.

If it gives you paragraphs instead of the visual board, say:

> **"Make it the interactive board — the quadrant with clickable items, verdict stamps, and my 30-day checklist."**

## Step 4 — Argue with it (10 min — do not skip this)

The first board is a strong draft, not gospel. This step is where it becomes *yours*:

> **"Challenge round: I think [item] is ranked too [high/low] because [reason]. Defend the placement or move it."**

Do this for at least two items. Also ask:

> **"What's the one item on this board you'd bet I abandon first, and what would stop that?"**

That answer is usually worth the whole exercise.

## Step 5 — Do the first move within 48 hours

Your #1 START item comes with a first move and a ready-to-run AI prompt. Copy the prompt into the same chat and run it *now*, while you're motivated. If the output isn't right, say **"closer to [what you want] — try again"** — iterating is the whole skill of using AI, and this is your first rep.

Then put the next move on your actual calendar with a date. A move without a date doesn't exist.

## Step 6 — Come back in two weeks

Open a new chat (paste the skill again if you used the paste method) and say:

> **"Two-week check-in on my board. Here's what I did: [list]. Here's what I didn't: [list]. Re-score and tell me the truth."**

The re-score is where momentum compounds — done items move, avoided items get called out, and the next three moves regenerate.

---

## The fine print (read once)

- **Verify local requirements yourself.** Licenses, permits, insurance, food-safety rules vary by city and state — the board will flag these, not decide them.
- **No big irreversible moves as first steps.** Nothing on your board should say "quit your job" or "sign a lease" in week one. If it does, push back — first moves generate evidence, not risk.
- **This is a planning tool, not a licensed professional.** For legal, tax, or financial decisions with real stakes, the board tells you *when* to see a pro — then see one.
